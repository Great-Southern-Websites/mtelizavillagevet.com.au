About our clinic

Your local village vet

Mt Eliza Village Vet is a small, family-owned and operated clinic run by husband-and-wife veterinarians Drs Pete and Libby Sorensen. Since opening in 2016, our team has been dedicated to a high standard of modern, personal and compassionate care, with a fully operational theatre for soft tissue and orthopaedic surgery right here in the village.
