Mt Eliza Village Vet is a small, family-owned and operated clinic at **85 Canadian Bay Road, Mount Eliza**, run by husband-and-wife veterinarians **Drs Pete and Libby Sorensen**.

The practice opened in **2016**, after Pete and Libby pursued a range of veterinary paths, from mixed practice to emergency medicine and surgery, and decided to bring that experience home to the village where Pete grew up.

![Mt Eliza Village Vet clinic on Canadian Bay Road](/images/clinic-1.jpg)

## Our team

We're a team of ten: four veterinarians, five nurses and our receptionist Corinna. Everyone here is dedicated to providing a high standard of modern, personal and compassionate care for our patients, and we actively support ongoing professional development, so our skills keep growing with the profession.

[Meet the whole team →](/team)

## Our facilities

The clinic offers routine preventative care, dental services, desexing, diagnostics and surgery, with a **fully operational theatre for soft tissue and orthopaedic surgery**, including advanced [TPLO surgery](/services/tplo) performed in-house. We also run [puppy pre-school](/services/puppy-preschool) classes.

We care for dogs, cats, rabbits, birds, reptiles and guinea pigs, and we [help injured wildlife](/wildlife) too.
