Pet insurance is becoming more and more popular in Australia. It offers peace of mind by covering some or all of the treatment costs if your pet is involved in an accident or suffers a sudden illness.

Unlike human healthcare, there's no Medicare equivalent for pets, and modern veterinary treatment can add up quickly. 24-hour emergency and critical care can cost over $1,000 per day, and surgery for a fractured bone or an exploratory procedure can run to thousands of dollars before recovery care begins.

Insurance means you never have to make a difficult decision about your pet's care based on cost alone.
