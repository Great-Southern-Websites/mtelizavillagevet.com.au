If your dog has a torn ACL (cruciate ligament) or an unstable knee, we offer **TPLO (Tibial Plateau Levelling Osteotomy) surgery in-house**. Performed by Dr Peter, this advanced technique changes the mechanics of the knee so your dog can bear weight comfortably again — with a typically rapid recovery compared to traditional techniques.

## What it costs

TPLO procedures range from approximately **$4,500–$5,900**, including the surgery, a day in hospital, post-op medications, follow-up checks and repeat X-rays eight weeks after the surgery. See our [price list](/pricing) for other orthopaedic options, including extracapsular cruciate surgery.

## The day of surgery

### Before surgery

Your dog is fasted overnight and admitted between 8–9am. We perform a pre-surgical assessment including blood work and X-rays, place an IV catheter and prepare a tailored anaesthetic plan.

### During surgery

The surgeon makes an incision, levels the tibial plateau and stabilises the bone with a plate and screws. The surgery typically takes between 1 and 2.5 hours, depending on the complexity and the size of the dog.

### After surgery and home care

Your dog recovers from the anaesthetic in-hospital with full pain management, and most go home the same day. You'll receive medication instructions and activity restrictions, plus scheduled follow-up visits at 4 days, 10–14 days and 8 weeks post-op.
