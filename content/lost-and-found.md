## We can help too

If you become stuck, you can bring a lost pet to us at the clinic. We'll scan for a microchip and contact the Shire to give the pet the very best chance of being reunited with their family.

<div class="note"><strong>Tip:</strong> keeping your pet's microchip details up to date is the single best way to make sure a lost pet finds their way home. Not sure if yours are current? <a href="/contact">Ask us to check at your next visit</a>.</div>
