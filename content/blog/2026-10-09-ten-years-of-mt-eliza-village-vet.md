September marked a special milestone for us: ten years of caring for our local community and their much-loved pets.

![Mt Eliza Village Vet, ten years of care, community and furry friends, with a thank you to the local community]({=site.url('images/uploads/mt-eliza-village-vet-10-years-of-care-2.png')})

It began with acquiring the property on Canadian Bay Road and undertaking extensive renovations to turn it into a veterinary clinic. Watching it grow into the practice it is today has been something to be proud of.

Over the past decade we have built lasting relationships with our clients, cared for countless furry family members, and worked alongside a wonderful team of dedicated veterinarians, nurses and receptionists.

Looking back on the past ten years, we would like to extend a heartfelt thank you to our families for their unwavering support, to our staff past and present, and to our local community for putting their trust in us. We are incredibly proud of what we have achieved together, and we look forward to continuing to provide compassionate, high-quality veterinary care for many years to come.

Thank you for being part of it. Here is to the next ten years.
