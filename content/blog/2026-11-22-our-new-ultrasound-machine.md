Libby and I have always placed great importance on continuing education and keeping up with the latest advances in veterinary medicine.

One of the things we have valued most about starting Mt Eliza Village Vet is having the freedom to shape the direction of our clinic, from the services we offer to the equipment we invest in, always with the goal of providing the best possible care for our patients.

Ultrasound has become an invaluable tool in modern veterinary medicine, particularly in general practice. From assessing heart function and examining abdominal organs to guiding tissue sampling, it plays an important role in diagnosing and managing a wide range of conditions.

Over the years we have invested considerable time in developing and refining our abdominal and cardiac ultrasound skills, and last week we were very excited to welcome our brand new ultrasound machine to the clinic: a Siemens Acuson Sequoia with four different probes.

![Drs Pete and Libby Sorensen standing beside the clinic's new Siemens Acuson Sequoia ultrasound machine]({=site.url('images/uploads/us-ls-and-ps.jpg')})

Thanks to Earl at Atlas Diagnostics for getting us set up.

It is an exciting investment for us and another step forward in our commitment to providing high-quality veterinary care to our patients and community.
