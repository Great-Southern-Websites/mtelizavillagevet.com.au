Some good news from the nursing team. Sarah Muir has completed her Certificate IV in Veterinary Nursing in 2025, and she now joins our other Cert IV qualified nurses at the clinic.

Sarah joined Mt Eliza Village Vet in 2020. She completed her Certificate III in Animal Studies first, then went on to the Cert IV while working with us. Anyone who has brought a pet in over the last few years will most likely have met her. She adores all animals big and small, including her own cats Tabitha and Tortellini.

We have updated Sarah's profile on the [team page]({=site.url('team')}) to reflect her new qualification. You can also read about the rest of the vets and nurses there.

Congratulations Sarah from everyone at MEVV.
