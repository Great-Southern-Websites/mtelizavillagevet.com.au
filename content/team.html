Our veterinarians

Dr Peter Sorensen

Veterinarian & Practice Owner · BVSc, MANZCVS (Surg)

Pete has lived on the Mornington Peninsula most of his life and went to school right here in Mt Eliza. A University of Melbourne graduate (2008), he completed surgery membership exams in 2013 and performs soft tissue and orthopaedic surgery — including TPLO — in-house. He also has a soft spot for reptile medicine.

Dr Libby Sorensen

Veterinarian & Practice Owner · BVSc, MANZCVS (E&CC)

Libby graduated from the University of Melbourne in 2009 and completed Emergency and Critical Care membership exams in 2013. She manages the practice while studying diagnostic imaging, and offers basic echocardiography — particularly for dogs with mitral valve disease.

Dr Beth Metcalfe

Veterinarian · BSc, DVM

Born in England, raised on the Peninsula, Beth graduated from the University of Melbourne in 2019. Her interests include antimicrobial stewardship and One Health research. Off duty she runs, plays field hockey and hangs out with her greyhound, Margot.

Dr Lyla Wilson

Veterinarian · BSc, DVM

Lyla joined us after a stint in mixed-animal practice in East Gippsland. A University of Melbourne graduate (2023), she brings a warm, compassionate approach to every patient. She shares her home with Walter the cavoodle and Althea the cat.

Dr Megan Smith

Veterinarian · BVSc

With over 25 years of experience, Megan provides care rooted in deep clinical knowledge and genuine compassion. She takes a holistic approach spanning pain management, behaviour, musculoskeletal health and acupuncture, with a special passion for geriatric and anxious pets.

Our nurses & support team

Julia Groves

Senior Veterinary Nurse · Cert IV

Jules is an experienced vet nurse of 25+ years who joined MEVV in 2021. She primarily works in surgery and is a fountain of puppy advice, with a decade of cavoodle breeding experience behind her.

Lauren McAuley

Veterinary Nurse · Cert IV

With us since 2018, Lauren has special interests in wildlife and avian care and has volunteered at Australia Zoo with reptiles and birds. Ask her about her lorikeet, Birb.

Sarah Muir

Veterinary Nurse

Sarah joined in 2020, completed her Certificate III in Animal Studies and is studying her Cert IV in Veterinary Nursing. She adores all animals big and small — including her cats Tabitha and Tortellini.

Alex Tindal

Veterinary Nurse · Cert IV

Corinna Dillon

Receptionist

Sienna Botha
